The sourdough story

Good bread
takes time.

Made for those who appreciate bread with a little more character.

Sourdough at mio·rie begins with a carefully curated blend of premium European flours and stone-ground whole grains, including spelt and rye. Each grain brings its own unique characteristics, contributing to the depth of flavour, texture and complexity in our bread.

We then give our dough time.

Slow-fermented under controlled temperatures for an average of 40 hours, the process is intentional and never rushed. The long, cold maturation allows the deepening of flavour, giving each loaf its distinctive sourdough character.

From rustic classics to enriched specialty bakes with bold pairings, each loaf is mindfully crafted for that satisfying bite.

Rustic tradition. Naturally leavened. And very much a labour of love.

The pastry story

Small treats.
Simple pleasures.

A collection of small treats, made for leisurely afternoons over your favourite brew.

From French Gourmet Financiers with their delicate browned-butter aroma and vibrant toppings, and tender Sweet Madeleines finished with fine chocolate, to our square-cut Savoury Scones featuring layers of melted cheese and fresh herbs, each brings its own quiet pleasure to the table.

Made from scratch with fine selected ingredients, including French butter, French couverture chocolate, and Uji matcha and hojicha, our bakes honour familiar traditions with a little modern creativity.

Made with brown butter and almond flour, our financiers bring together familiar favourites and seasonal discoveries. Flavours are released seasonally, subject to availability.

Thoughtfully made. To savour slowly.

Little mio·rie indulgence

Two expressions.
One little indulgence.

An elegant favourite at our own table.

Baileys Classic Tiramisu. Italian ladyfingers soaked in espresso and Baileys Espresso Crème, layered with velvety mascarpone cream lightened with French cream. Rich yet delicate, the mellow bitterness of coffee balances the gentle sweetness of the cream, while a warm whisper of dark rum adds depth. Finished with a generous dusting of dark Valrhona cocoa.

Matcha-Misu. Layers of Italian ladyfingers soaked in Uji matcha, nestled between silky matcha-infused Italian mascarpone custard cream, folded with French whipping cream for a delicate, airy finish. Earthy and gently bittersweet, with subtle umami notes rounding off the richness of the cream.

Made for lingering moments, quiet celebrations or that occasional treat.
