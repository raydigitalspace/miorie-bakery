Who we are

Quality that speaks quietly.

At mio·rie, we believe good things take time.

Rooted in a love for thoughtful baking, our micro-boulangerie brings together carefully selected ingredients and a respect for the craft.

From naturally leavened breads to little treats and sweet indulgences, each creation begins with a simple intention: to make something worth savouring.

True to our ingredients, thoughtful in our craft, intentional in every creation.
