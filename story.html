How it began

From a loaf
to mio·rie.

Six years ago, I went hunting for a good rustic loaf for a dinner I was hosting. Finding none, I went home and baked my first overnight no-knead bread.

My home never smelled this good. My guests noticed it before they even sat down.

And I thought: if instant yeast can smell this good, sourdough must be heavenly.

Down the rabbit hole I went.

Six years, countless loaves, failed starters, revived starters, under-fermented and over-proofed dough, sticky, soupy mess, experiments of sorts, and plenty of bread my family bravely ate in the name of “research”, later, here we are.

I started sharing my sourdough with friends, and of late, friends of friends. All this while, dismissing a persistent thought floating in my head: why not share with a wider pool of people who may appreciate good bread?

So, after years of baking, learning and refining, I’m finally making it official.

Meet mio·rie, my little micro-boulangerie.

Our bread philosophy

More than
flavour.

For us, sourdough has always been about more than flavour.

We believe bread can be something to enjoy regularly while still being thoughtful about how it is made. Personally, I would be hard pressed to choose refined bread as an everyday staple if it weren’t for sourdough.

That is why we take a different approach to fermentation.

Our doughs are given long, slow and careful temperature-controlled fermentation, often averaging 40 hours. We give the natural fermentation process time to work through the dough rather than rushing it simply for the sake of production efficiency.

That approach also dictates the flour we use. Our signature blend isn’t built around a single flour or a predetermined recipe. It is curated from premium European flours and whole grains, including stone-ground spelt and rye, chosen for how their individual characteristics work together and, importantly, how they perform through an extended fermentation.

The result is bread with depth, character and a satisfying bite. More importantly, bread made with intention.

Because for us, good bread isn’t just about what goes into it. It’s also about giving it the time to become what it should be.

The sourdough story

Good bread
takes time.

Made for those who appreciate bread with a little more character.

Sourdough at mio·rie begins with a carefully curated blend of premium European flours and stone-ground whole grains, including spelt and rye. Each grain brings its own unique characteristics, creating depth, texture and flavour that goes beyond a single-flour loaf.

We then give the dough time.

Each loaf is naturally slow-fermented for an average of 40 hours. The process is intentional and never rushed. The long, cold maturation allows the live cultures to work their magic, developing a distinctive sourdough character, while potentially improving bioavailability.

From rustic everyday classics to the enriched specialty collection, each is thoughtfully crafted around the ingredients, fermentation and texture we want to bring to the table.

Rustic tradition, thoughtfully crafted.

One collection, two expressions. Our Artisan collection brings together enriched sourdough loaves and specialty bakes that venture beyond the everyday, pairing the depth of natural fermentation with richer doughs, distinctive flavour combinations and seasonal inspiration.

Made for those who love a softer, more tender loaf with a richer character, our enriched doughs incorporate eggs, cultured butter and a touch of honey, creating a delicate golden crust and a lofty, brioche-like crumb. Extended fermentation, often exceeding 40 hours, develops depth of flavour alongside the richness of the dough.

From rum-infused figs and pecans or kirsch-infused cherries to dark chocolate, coffee and orange, the specialty bakes in this collection explore bold pairings and distinctive inclusions in unexpected ways. Released in limited seasonal runs, each creation draws its character from the interplay of fruit, chocolate, spices and complementary flavours.

Made with intention: each loaf is an expression of our approach to the art of sourdough.

Small batches · a personal touch

Our principle

A little room for
a personal touch.

Intentional, thoughtfully crafted, with a little room for a personal touch.

A micro-bakery has the opportunity to offer something that larger, commercial bakery chains may find harder to provide: a personal conversation, some level of mutual understanding, and a little flexibility where it makes sense.

You get to know your customers, their flavour preferences and nuances, and you get to share stories.

Not every request needs to become a customisation. Sometimes, if a request can be met through a small adjustment that is straightforward without compromising the recipe, quality or production workflow, attempts can be made to meet it, where feasible.

At mio·rie, small-batch baking allows for a more personal approach. Where practical, we’re happy to consider small adjustments to suit individual preferences, while staying true to the integrity of each recipe and the care that goes into making it.
