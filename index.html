OUR STORY

A beautiful name.
A thoughtful little bakery.

Welcome to mio·rie — a micro-boulangerie inspired by the beauty of slow-baking, and the pleasure of something made with care.

Read more about our name ↓
Why mio·rie?

Pronounced “mee-oh-ree”, the name brings together mio (美), meaning beautiful, or perfect, with rie, a nod to the craft of the boulangerie.

Every loaf, pastry and treat is handcrafted in small batches by your local boulangère, with care, intention and thoughtfully selected ingredients. From my home kitchen to your table, crafted for simple, everyday pleasure.

Rustic tradition. Naturally leavened.

THE SOURDOUGH STORY

Good bread
takes time.

For our sourdough, time is an ingredient. Long, cold fermentation brings depth of flavour, texture and that distinctive sourdough character.

Read more about our bread ↓
Our approach to sourdough

Our dough is slow-fermented under controlled temperatures for an average of 40 hours. Long, cold maturation deepens the flavour. The process is intentional and never rushed.

A carefully curated blend of premium European flours and stone-ground whole grains, including spelt and rye, gives each loaf its character.

Meet the ingredients ↓

WHAT GOES INTO OUR SOURDOUGH

Every ingredient
has a part to play.

Start with the grain.
Discover the details, one ingredient at a time.

European flours & whole grains

A curated blend of bread flour, organic whole wheat flour, spelt and rye forms the foundation of our sourdough. Each grain contributes to the flavour, texture and complexity of the loaf.

Our sourdough starter

Our loaves are naturally leavened with sourdough starter. Given time through long, cold maturation, the dough develops its distinctive tang and depth of flavour.

Butter, eggs & specialty pairings

Our Artisan Butter Loaf is enriched with eggs and French butter, with a delicate finish of honey. Other specialty bakes explore pairings such as rum-infused Turkish figs and house-activated pecans. Each loaf has its own ingredient list in our shop.
