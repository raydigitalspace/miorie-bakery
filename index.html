Bread care

Keeping your loaf at its best.

A naturally fermented loaf, baked in small batches. A little care keeps it lovely.

  • Keep

    Slice what you’ll enjoy within 2 days and keep it in the bread bag, at room temperature.

  • Freeze

    Freeze the rest in a sealed bag, with baking paper between slices.

  • Reheat fresh

    Toast for 3 minutes, or warm at 180°C for 4–5 minutes in an oven or air fryer.

  • Reheat frozen

    Use the toaster’s defrost setting for 3–4 minutes, or spritz with water and warm at 180°C for 5–8 minutes.

Good to know

Before you order.

How far ahead should I order?

About four days for sourdough. Available dates are shown at checkout.

Where do I collect?

In eastern Singapore. The address comes with your order confirmation.

Is everything available every week?

Sourdough and madeleines are baked to order. Tiramisu is made by pre-order; we confirm your order once the batch is set. Financiers and scones are seasonal drops, announced here and in our shop.

Allergies or dietary needs?

“Read more” under each bake offers a glimpse of the flavours. A more detailed ingredient list is in our store on Take App. Please message us or leave a note at checkout for any allergen enquiries.
