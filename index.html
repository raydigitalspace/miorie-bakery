THE SOURDOUGH STORY

Good bread
takes time.

For our sourdough, time is an ingredient. Long, cold fermentation brings depth of flavour, texture and that distinctive sourdough character.

Read our sourdough story

Our dough is slow-fermented under controlled temperatures for an average of 40 hours. Long, cold maturation deepens the flavour. The process is intentional and never rushed.

A carefully curated blend of premium European flours and stone-ground whole grains, including spelt and rye, gives each loaf its character.

THE SPECIALTY COLLECTION

Artisan sourdough.

Enriched & specialty bakes. Eggs and French butter in our croissant-inspired Butter Loaf; sun-dried Turkish figs infused with dark rum and house-activated pecans in our Fig & Pecan. Distinctive ingredients, with a sourdough character of their own.

Read more about the ingredients & craft

Artisan Butter Loaf

Enriched with eggs and French butter, our signature croissant-inspired loaf is laminated with frozen grated butter, baked to a golden crust with a soft, brioche-like crumb, and finished with just a delicate kiss of honey.

Rum-infused Fig & Pecan

Built on our curated blend of European flours and whole grains, this loaf features a bold and beautiful pairing—sun-dried Turkish figs infused with dark rum, and house-activated pecans slow-dried and lightly toasted with butter for a clean crunch.

The flour foundation

Our sourdough begins with a carefully curated blend of premium European flours and stone-ground whole grains, including spelt and rye.

An elegant favourite at our own table, with Italian ladyfingers soaked in espresso and Baileys Espresso Crème, layered with velvety mascarpone cream lightened with French cream.

Matcha-Misu

S$20.00 per 12 oz tub

A refined matcha interpretation of the Italian classic.

Read more

Layers of Italian ladyfingers soaked in Uji matcha, nestled between silky matcha-infused Italian mascarpone custard cream, folded with French whipping cream for a delicate, airy finish.

Earthy and gently bittersweet, with subtle umami notes rounding off the richness of the cream. Each spoonful brings a quiet balance of depth and lightness, finished with a fine dusting of matcha for a delicate savoury lift.

A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Before you order.

How far ahead should I order?

For sourdough, please allow approximately four days from order confirmation for pickup. See the Take App shop for current availability and preorder details.

Where do I collect my bakes?

Pickup is in eastern Singapore. Please refer to the shop and your order confirmation for the exact arrangements.

Are all the treats available every week?

Tiramisu is open for preorder. Financiers and scones are seasonal releases. Check the Take App store announcements for the latest menu.

Where can I check ingredients or allergens?

Read the individual product information in our Take App shop and contact the baker through the shop before ordering if you have any dietary or allergy questions.
