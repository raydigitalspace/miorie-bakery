Enriched with eggs and French butter, our signature croissant-inspired loaf is laminated with frozen grated butter, baked to a golden crust with a soft, brioche-like crumb, and finished with just a delicate kiss of honey.

A few things to know

Before you order.

How far ahead should I order?

For sourdough, please allow about four days from order to pickup. Available dates are shown at checkout.

Where do I collect my bakes?

Pickup is in eastern Singapore. The exact address is sent with your order confirmation.

Are all the treats available every week?

Sourdough and tiramisu are baked to order. Madeleines and savoury scones are seasonal drops, announced here and on our shop.

How do I keep and reheat my sourdough?

Slice and store what you’ll enjoy within 2 days in the bread bag at room temperature. Freeze remaining slices in a sealed bag or container, placing baking paper between slices to prevent sticking.

Fresh: toast for 3 minutes, or warm in an air fryer or pre-heated oven at 180°C for 4–5 minutes. Frozen: use the toaster’s defrost setting for 3–4 minutes, or spritz with water and warm at 180°C for 5–8 minutes.

Where can I check ingredients or allergens?

Each bake’s “Read more” lists its key ingredients. If you have an allergy or dietary question, please contact us before ordering.
